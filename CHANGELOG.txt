  imported; you can assign players later.

  scan is running, changing the lineups still requires stopping that scan.

TEAM SIDE SWAP DURING SOLOQ DOWNLOAD
- Blue/Red swap now works even while Riot downloads continue in background.
- Teams, logos, current profile summaries and finished champion statistics
  move together. Later updates are saved to the CURRENT side even if
  the scan started before the swap.
- Downloads, progress and SQLite match cache remain intact.
- A standard settings SAVE remains disabled during an active scan to avoid
  changing the actual player roster while that scan is running.
